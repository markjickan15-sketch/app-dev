# My Favorite Movie: Chronicle

**Chronicle** is a *found footage science fiction* movie released in **2012**.  
It tells the story of three friends who gain superpowers after discovering a mysterious object underground.

---

## Main Characters
1. **Andrew Detmer** – a shy and bullied teenager who becomes corrupted by power  
2. **Matt Garetty** – Andrew cousin who tries to guide him  
3. **Steve Montgomery** – a popular and friendly student
